07 — FAQ

Good to know
before we begin.

A few quick answers to common project questions.

What services do you offer?

I offer YouTube thumbnails, social media posts, product advertising, AI reels, video editing, presentations, research documents, website development and mobile app development.

How do I get a quote for my project?

Share your requirements, references and expected deliverables through email or WhatsApp. I'll review the details and provide a custom quote.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the type and complexity of the project. I'll confirm an estimated delivery time before we begin.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I can discuss projects with clients and businesses from different countries.

Can I request changes to the final work?

Yes. The revision scope can be discussed and agreed upon before the project starts.

How can I start a project?

Contact me through email or WhatsApp with a short description of what you need. I'll get back to you to discuss the next steps.
